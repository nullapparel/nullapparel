DEFINED BY NOTHING

THE MANIFESTO & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

// 01. WHO WE ARE

NULL is an independent, minimalist streetwear concept rooted in absolute restraint. We reject commercial noise, loud branding, and mass-market overproduction. In a world oversaturated with excessive graphics and trends, NULL stands for pure form, architectural lines, and absolute silence.

Every piece is crafted exclusively in black—deliberately stripped of unnecessary elements to let the silhouette and quality speak for themselves.

// 02. THE 60 PIECES / YEAR RULE

True luxury lies in scarcity and deliberate choice. We operate on a strict operational limit of producing only 60 total pieces per year, partitioned across specialized drops and phases. Once a drop concludes, the ledger closes permanently. No restocks. No mass scaling.
